# Research notes: Meta Superintelligence Labs talent raid

- Meta invested $14.3B for a 49% stake in Scale AI, June 2025; Alexandr Wang (Scale AI founder/CEO) became Meta's Chief AI Officer, leading the new Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meta_Superintelligence_Labs
- Nat Friedman (ex-GitHub CEO) joined to lead AI products; Daniel Gross (ex-Safe Superintelligence CEO) joined July 2025 as Friedman's counterpart. Friedman/Gross previously ran investment fund NFDG. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meta_Superintelligence_Labs ; https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/meta-poaches-safe-superintelligence-ceo-for-new-ai-team/493604
- Shengjia Zhao, OpenAI co-creator of ChatGPT, named MSL chief scientist July 25, 2025. https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/25/zuckerberg-shengjia-zhao-meta-ai-lab-chief-scientist-openai.html ; https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/25/meta-names-shengjia-zhao-as-chief-scientist-of-ai-superintelligence-unit
- Ruoming Pang, who led Apple's ~100-person Apple Intelligence model team (15 years at Google before Apple, joined Apple 2021), hired by Meta July 7, 2025, at "tens of millions" in annual compensation. https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/meta-offers-apple-manager-tens-of-millions-to-join-ai-team/494354
- Broader hiring spree pushed AI-engineer pay industrywide, per DeepLearning.AI's The Batch. https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/metas-hiring-spree-pushes-up-salaries-for-ai-engineers-across-the-industry
- Meta paused AI hiring in August 2025 after the poaching spree. https://www.techcrunch.com/2025/08/21/report-meta-is-hitting-pause-on-ai-hiring-after-its-poaching-spree/
- August 2025 restructuring split MSL into four groups: TBD Lab (frontier LLMs, led by Wang), FAIR (research), Products and Applied Research (consumer, led by Friedman), MSL Infra (led by Aparna Ramani). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meta_Superintelligence_Labs
- Oct. 22, 2025: Meta cut about 600 jobs from the "bloated" AI unit as Wang consolidated control; Axios and CNBC both covered the reorg same day. https://www.axios.com/2025/10/22/meta-superintelligence-tbd-ai-reorg ; https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/22/meta-layoffs-ai.html
- Yann LeCun, Meta's chief AI scientist, departed Nov. 20, 2025, to found Advanced Machine Intelligence Labs (AMI Labs), a world-model startup built on V-JEPA architecture, led by CEO Alex LeBrun with LeCun as executive chair; reported Jan. 3, 2026. Quote used: "LLMs basically are a dead end when it comes to superintelligence" (safe, no banned words; other LeCun quotes found contain "don't" and are excluded). https://the-decoder.com/you-certainly-dont-tell-a-researcher-like-me-what-to-do-says-lecun-as-he-exits-meta-for-his-own-startup/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meta_Superintelligence_Labs
- April 8, 2026: MSL shipped Muse Spark, first model in the Muse family, powering the Meta AI assistant — evidence MSL kept shipping despite churn. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meta_Superintelligence_Labs

Angle: read hiring/restructuring/departure sequence as strategy — MSL bought a research organization chart in one summer, then spent a year discovering the chart does not run itself.

## Coinbase's AI-driven reorganization and Rob Witoff's CTO promotion
- July 28, 2026: Rob Witoff named Coinbase's new CTO. https://crypto.news/coinbase-names-new-cto-after-14-workforce-cut/
- Witoff's background: joined Coinbase 2014, worked there to 2017 leading security and infrastructure and becoming chief architect. Founded institutional crypto-custody company Unit 410, later acquired by Coinbase. Returned to Coinbase December 2024 as head of platform, then became CTO July 28, 2026.
- May 2026: Coinbase cut approximately 14% of its workforce (roughly 700 roles), flattened management, and reorganized teams around AI-assisted development tools. CEO Brian Armstrong said AI let engineers complete in days work that previously took teams weeks.
- By mid-2026, nearly all newly merged code at Coinbase was AI-generated and human-reviewed, up from 5.7% in Q1 2025.
- Other executive moves in the same window: Chief Legal Officer Paul Grewal gave notice July 8, 2026 that he would depart July 31; Molly Abraham expected to become general counsel/corporate secretary; Grewal to advise through October.
Source: crypto.news, "Coinbase names new CTO after 14% workforce cut," https://crypto.news/coinbase-names-new-cto-after-14-workforce-cut/

## Sriram Krishnan: a16z crypto investor to White House AI policy
- a16z general partner (Feb. 2021-Dec. 2024), investing across consumer, enterprise and Web3; led the firm's first international office (London) starting 2023.
- Dec. 22, 2024: announced as incoming White House Senior Policy Advisor for Artificial Intelligence (Trump administration); served Jan. 2025-June 30, 2026.
- Co-authored the American AI Action Plan (July 2025) and conducted AI diplomacy with international partners including Saudi Arabia.
- Joined the National Economic Council in 2026 following his AI-advisor departure.
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sriram_Krishnan

## Angle
Talent crosses the crypto-AI corridor in two directions at once: crypto-native operators (the Hafner brothers, ex-Bittensor) build AI companies using token-funding playbooks they learned in crypto, while AI-adjacent policy and technical talent (Sriram Krishnan) cycles out of crypto-focused venture capital into AI governance at the highest level of government. Meanwhile, established crypto exchanges like Coinbase now restructure their own engineering organizations, and even executive suites, around AI tooling, making the corridor bidirectional and structural rather than a handful of anecdotal hires.

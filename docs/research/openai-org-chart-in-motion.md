# Research notes: OpenAI's org chart in motion

- Fidji Simo, OpenAI's Applications CEO (joined 2025 from Instacart), announced July 9, 2026, she would step down from full-time duty to a part-time advisory role after a relapse of a neuroimmune condition first disclosed in April 2026. Brad Lightcap (COO), Sarah Friar (CFO) and Kevin Weil (CPO, since departed) had reported to her; TechCrunch names no permanent successor. Sam Altman posted on X: "i am really sad about this and very grateful for all fidji has done for openai." https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/fidji-simo-steps-down-from-openais-no-2-role/
- Fidji Simo co-founded ChronicleBio, an AI-driven biotech startup analyzing blood data to research chronic disease (including her own POTS condition), which raised $15 million; profiled by Fortune Aug. 3, Aug. 4 and Aug. 10, 2026. https://fortune.com/2026/08/10/fidji-simo-on-her-life-after-openai-new-startup-chroniclebio/ ; https://dealroom.co/news/144187-fidji-simos-chroniclebio-raises-15m-to-build-an-internet-of-biology-from/
- Kevin Weil (ex-Twitter/Instagram, joined OpenAI as chief product officer, later led OpenAI for Science) departed OpenAI April 17, 2026, the same day Bill Peebles (Sora video-model researcher) exited; OpenAI called Sora and OpenAI for Science "side quests" being wound down as the company refocuses on enterprise AI. Srinivas Narayanan, CTO of Enterprise Applications, also left around this time, citing family time. Weil posted on X that "OpenAI for Science is being decentralized into other research teams." https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/17/kevin-weil-and-bill-peebles-exit-openai-as-company-continues-to-shed-side-quests/
- Noam Shazeer, Google DeepMind's Gemini co-lead (rejoined Google in 2024 via the Character.AI license-and-hire deal reportedly worth $2.7 billion), left Google for OpenAI, reported June 18, 2026, by CNBC and Axios. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/18/google-gemini-co-lead-noam-shazeer-leaves-for-openai.html ; https://www.axios.com/2026/06/18/noam-shazeer-google-openai-characterai
- OpenAI completed its for-profit recapitalization Oct. 28, 2025: the OpenAI Foundation (nonprofit) retains a controlling stake and oversight of the new public benefit corporation, OpenAI Group PBC. https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/28/openai-completes-its-for-profit-recapitalization ; https://fortune.com/2025/10/28/openai-for-profit-restructuring-microsoft-stake
- CNBC, Aug. 14, 2026: "OpenAI talent exodus raises 'huge red flag' ahead of IPO" — headline-level evidence executive churn is being read as an IPO risk signal (paywalled; used title/date only, no fetch). https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/14/open-ai-ipo-red-flag.html
- CNBC, April 17, 2026: "OpenAI loses multiple executives in latest leadership shakeup" — corroborates the Weil/Peebles/Narayanan cluster of departures. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/17/openai-executives-leave.html
- Stable 2026 leadership roles (general knowledge, consistent with search snippets, not freshly fetched): Sarah Friar, CFO; Brad Lightcap, COO; Chris Lehane, Chief Global Affairs Officer; Jakub Pachocki, Chief Scientist; Mark Chen, Chief Research Officer.

Angle: OpenAI's 2026 org chart shows a company shedding "side quests" (Sora as independent bet, OpenAI for Science, Applications leadership continuity) to concentrate on enterprise AI and frontier research, while still importing marquee research talent (Shazeer) — a roadmap signal distinct from Meta's raid-then-restructure pattern.

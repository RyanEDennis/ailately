## OpenAI — confirmed IPO filing and restructuring
- PBC restructuring: OpenAI proposed converting from capped-profit to a Delaware public benefit corporation in December 2024; completed the transition Oct. 28, 2025, after approval from the California and Delaware attorneys general. Resulting ownership: OpenAI Foundation 26%, Microsoft 27%, employees and other investors 47%.
- "On June 8, 2026, OpenAI confirmed it filed for an IPO with the US Securities and Exchange Commission."
- Sam Altman told staff he expected an IPO "within the next year," and noted that progress on recursive self-improvement could make delaying an IPO advantageous.
- Sarah Friar: Chief Financial Officer, former Nextdoor CEO, former CFO at Block Inc.
- Source: Wikipedia "OpenAI," fetched, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI

## Precedent: CoreWeave (reused from article 23 research)
- IPO March 28, 2025, ~$27 billion valuation, $1.5 billion raised (cut from a planned $2.7B). Contract backlog reached $104 billion by Q3 2026 (per verified assignment context / investors.coreweave.com). A neocloud, not a frontier lab, but the closest available precedent for how public markets price AI-infrastructure exposure.
- Source: Wikipedia "CoreWeave," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CoreWeave

## Cerebras — withdrawn, still intends to go public
- Filed S-1 for Nasdaq (ticker CBRS) September 2024.
- October 2024: CFIUS began reviewing G42's investment in Cerebras, a review that risked delaying the IPO.
- May 2025: CEO Andrew Feldman said the company hoped to go public in 2025.
- September 2025: raised $1.1 billion in a Series G round, valuing Cerebras at $8.1 billion.
- October 2025: filed to withdraw its IPO registration; Feldman said the company still intends to go public.
- January 2026: announced a $10+ billion deal with OpenAI, described as "ahead of its IPO."
- Source: Wikipedia "Cerebras," fetched, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cerebras

## Databricks — staying private on mega-rounds instead
- August 2026: $190 billion post-money valuation after a $5 billion Series M led by Coatue Management, joined by Blackstone, MGX, T. Rowe Price, and Sixth Street Growth.
- Revenue run rate: $7 billion annualized as of August 2026.
- CEO Ali Ghodsi; stated in August 2026 that "AGI has already arrived."
- No CFO named in the fetched content.
- Source: Wikipedia "Databricks," fetched, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Databricks

## Klarna — completed IPO, useful contrast
- IPO completed Sept. 10, 2025, NYSE ticker KLAR, priced at $40 a share; offering comprised roughly 34.3 million shares, of which Klarna itself sold 5 million (the remainder sold by existing shareholders).
- Company confidentially began the IPO process in 2024, filed its public registration in March 2025, delayed in April 2025 amid tariff-driven market volatility, then relaunched later in 2025.
- Peak private valuation: $46 billion in a June 2021 SoftBank Vision Fund 2-led round — useful contrast, since the $40 IPO share price reportedly priced the company well under that 2021 peak (exact total valuation at IPO not reliably computed from the fetch; avoided asserting a specific total-valuation figure given uncertain extraction).
- CEO Sebastian Siemiatkowski; board chair Michael Moritz.
- Source: Wikipedia "Klarna," fetched, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klarna

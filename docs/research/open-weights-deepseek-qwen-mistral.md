- Qwen3 released April 28, 2025: dense variants 0.6B-32B parameters, MoE variants at 30B-A3B and 235B-A22B, trained on 36 trillion tokens across 119 languages, Apache 2.0 licensed (Qwen3-Max, a 1T+ parameter variant, stays proprietary). By May 2026 the Qwen app reported 234 million users; more than 200,000 Qwen model variants exist on Hugging Face, with Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct alone surpassing 18 million downloads. No specific Qwen team leads or researchers were named in the source — a notable contrast to Western labs' personality-driven coverage. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qwen

Arthur Mensch, CEO and co-founder (company founded April 2023), previously worked at Google DeepMind. 2026 moves: acquired Paris-based Koyeb and struck an Accenture enterprise partnership (February); raised $830 million for data centers near Paris and in Sweden (March); acquired Austrian industrial-simulation firm Emmi AI (May). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mistral_AI

Market-share proxies (Qwen's 234 million app users, 200,000+ Hugging Face variants; DeepSeek's App Store chart-topping) show the open tier converting into real usage, not just benchmark bragging rights, even as DeepSeek's own IPO prep signals its "open" identity increasingly coexists with conventional startup-finance ambitions.

# Research notes: Apple's AI exodus and the Ternus transition

- Ruoming Pang, Apple's top AI models executive (led the ~100-person team behind Apple Intelligence models), left for Meta Superintelligence Labs; Bloomberg reported it July 7, 2025. MacRumors reported "two more" Apple AI executives poached by Meta July 17, 2025. https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-07-07/apple-loses-its-top-ai-models-executive-to-meta-s-hiring-spree ; https://www.macrumors.com/2025/07/17/meta-poaches-two-more-apple-ai-executives/
- Ke Yang, head of Apple's AI search project, left for Meta; reported by MacRumors Oct. 16, 2025, and PYMNTS around the same period. https://www.macrumors.com/2025/10/16/apple-ai-search-head-leaves-for-meta/
- John Giannandrea, Apple's AI/ML chief, announced retirement via Apple's own newsroom in December 2025; officially departed the week of April 13, 2026, after a "resting and vesting" period (MacRumors, 9to5Mac). He joined AI science startup CuspAI in April 2026 (MacTech). https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/12/john-giannandrea-to-retire-from-apple/ ; https://www.macrumors.com/2026/04/13/john-giannandrea-departs-apple-this-week/ ; https://www.mactech.com/2026/04/28/apples-former-ai-chief-john-giannandrea-is-joining-a-science-ai-startup/
- Amar Subramanya named Giannandrea's successor to lead Apple's AI efforts; prior experience at Google (led Gemini Assistant efforts) and Microsoft (Azure AI). Giannandrea's former teams redistributed partly to COO Sabih Khan and services chief Eddy Cue. (fetched from WebProNews aggregation; no precise announcement date confirmed — used with that caveat.) https://www.webpronews.com/apple-ai-chief-john-giannandrea-to-retire-in-2026-successor-announced/
- Apple-Google Gemini/Siri deal: TechCrunch reported Jan. 12, 2026, that Google's Gemini would power Apple's AI features including Siri; MacRumors confirmed April 22, 2026 that Gemini-powered Siri was coming later in 2026; deal reportedly worth roughly $1 billion; unveiled publicly at WWDC 2026 (June 2026). https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/12/googles-gemini-to-power-apples-ai-features-like-siri/ ; https://www.macrumors.com/2026/04/22/google-gemini-powered-siri-2026/
- Apple's own newsroom (fetched), April 20, 2026: Tim Cook becomes Executive Chairman; John Ternus becomes CEO, effective Sept. 1, 2026. Ternus joined Apple's product design team in 2001, became VP of Hardware Engineering in 2013, SVP of Hardware Engineering in 2021 — hardware pedigree, not an AI/software background. Cook: Ternus has "the mind of an engineer... the heart to lead with integrity and honor" (paraphrased in piece per one-quote budget). Board chair Arthur Levinson also commented on the choice. https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/04/tim-cook-to-become-apple-executive-chairman-john-ternus-to-become-apple-ceo/
- TheStreet, Sept. 2, 2026 (fetched), "Apple's new CEO faces his first big test": Ternus's first-day memo called the Sept. 9 product launch "phenomenal" — an unusually bold claim against Apple's cautious-communication norm, creating a credibility test against softer growth/margin guidance. Piece does not discuss AI or Siri directly. https://www.thestreet.com/technology/apple-ceo-john-ternus-phenomenal-launch

Angle: Apple's 2025 AI brain drain (Pang, Yang, and others to Meta) forced a two-track patch — import outside AI leadership (Subramanya from Google/Microsoft) and license outside AI capability (Gemini for Siri) — rather than rebuild in-house from the departed core. The company then handed its CEO transition to a hardware engineer, not an AI leader, raising the real question the Ternus era must answer.

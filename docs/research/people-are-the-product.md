## Fidji Simo at OpenAI (joined, then departed)
Fetched https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/fidji-simo-steps-down-from-openais-no-2-role/. Simo joined OpenAI as CEO of Applications in May 2025, arriving from Instacart, where she had been CEO since 2021 (the hire itself reported earlier by The Register, May 8, 2025 — https://www.theregister.com/2025/05/08/openai_apps_chif_instacart/). She stepped down July 9, 2026, citing a relapse of a neuroimmune condition that extended a medical leave; she moves to a part-time advisory role. COO Brad Lightcap (who had reported to Simo) shifted to "special projects," and CPO Kevin Weil left the company entirely — a second, related org-chart move. Sam Altman quote per TechCrunch: "i am really sad about this and very grateful for all fidji has done for openai... we all wish her the best for a speedy recovery." Corroborated by Bloomberg (https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-09/openai-executive-fidji-simo-to-step-down-following-medical-leave) and Fortune (https://fortune.com/2026/07/10/fidji-simo-openai-exit-women-power-fragile-tech/).

Budget note: WebSearch stayed 0/200 session-wide. Verified facts:

- MIT NANDA, "The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025," published July 2025. Authors: Aditya Challapally, Chris Pease, Ramesh Raskar, Pradyumna Chari. Methodology: 52 structured interviews, 153 senior leaders surveyed across four industry conferences, analysis of 300+ publicly disclosed AI initiatives. Key finding, quoted: "95% of organizations are getting zero return" despite $30-40 billion in enterprise GenAI investment; only 5% of integrated pilots extract measurable value/reach production. Researchers attribute the gap primarily to implementation approach, not model quality or regulation. Source: mlq.ai/media/quarterly_decks/v0.1_State_of_AI_in_Business_2025_Report.pdf (fetched directly, primary source PDF).
- Gartner, "Gartner Predicts Over 40% of Agentic AI Projects Will Be Canceled by End of 2027," June 25, 2025. Reasons cited: escalating costs, unclear business value, inadequate risk controls. Analyst: Anushree Verma, Senior Director Analyst, Gartner, quoted: "Most agentic AI projects right now are early stage experiments or proof of concepts that are mostly driven by hype," and "Many use cases positioned as agentic today don't require agentic implementations." Also cites a January 2025 Gartner poll of 3,412 webinar attendees: 19% made significant agentic-AI investments, 42% conservative investments, 8% no investment, 31% undecided. Source: gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027 (fetched directly).
- Gartner separately, "Gartner Says Generative AI for Procurement Has Entered the Trough of Disillusionment," July 30, 2025; and The Economist, "Welcome to the AI trough of disillusionment," May 21, 2025 — both cited via Wikipedia's Generative artificial intelligence article for the framing that companies were "increasingly abandoning generative AI pilot projects" by mid-2025 over integration difficulties, data quality and unmet returns. URLs: gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-07-30-gartner-says-generative-ai-for-procurement-has-entered-the-trough-of-disillusionment; economist.com/business/2025/05/21/welcome-to-the-ai-trough-of-disillusionment.
- McKinsey, "The State of AI" (QuantumBlack), report dated Aug. 25, 2026: 37% of respondents attribute at least some EBIT impact to AI (unchanged from 2025); 6% are "AI high performers" (≥5% EBIT impact); 44% report AI scaling across the enterprise (up from 38% in 2025); 88% report regular AI use in at least one business function; 80% report improved individual productivity; 60% expect increased AI investment. Quote: "Organizations' conviction in AI is growing faster than the immediate financial returns they can attribute to it." Source: mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai (fetched directly).
- Deloitte, "State of AI in the Enterprise," 2026 report (fielded Aug-Sept 2025), survey of 3,235 senior leaders across 24 countries: 66% report productivity/efficiency gains; 53% enhanced insights/decision-making; 40% cost reduction; 20% report increased revenue versus 74% who aspire to it; only 34% use AI to "deeply transform" (new products/reinvented processes); companies with 40%+ of projects in production projected to double within six months. Source: deloitte.com/us/en/what-we-do/capabilities/applied-artificial-intelligence/content/state-of-generative-ai-in-enterprise.html (fetched directly).

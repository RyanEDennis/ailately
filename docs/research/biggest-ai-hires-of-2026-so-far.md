## Jeff Dean founds Discovery Loop (verified, huge)
- Aug. 5, 2026: Jeff Dean (Google Chief Scientist, 27-year Google veteran) departs to co-found Discovery Loop with Sanjay Ghemawat (Senior Fellow, Google), Oriol Vinyals (Research VP, Google DeepMind), and Quoc Le (co-founder, Google Brain). Company automates scientific research: "proposing experiments, running them, evaluating results, and iterating, thousands of times over." Google is a "founding investor" providing compute for at least the first year; Radical Ventures and Khosla Ventures also back it. No funding figure disclosed. Source: GeekWire, Aug. 5, 2026, https://www.geekwire.com/2026/the-startup-idea-that-convinced-a-uw-computer-science-legend-to-leave-google-after-27-years/; also TechCrunch, Aug. 5, 2026, https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/jeff-dean-and-other-top-ai-researchers-are-leaving-google-to-launch-their-own-startup/

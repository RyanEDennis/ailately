## Sept 2025 Nvidia-OpenAI LOI (the original $100B deal)
- Announced Sept. 22, 2025. Letter of intent: Nvidia intends to invest up to $100 billion in OpenAI, progressively as each gigawatt of capacity comes online. At least 10 gigawatts of Nvidia systems, first phase H2 2026 on the Vera Rubin platform. Nvidia named preferred strategic compute/networking partner; coordination named with Microsoft, Oracle, SoftBank, Stargate.
- Jensen Huang: "NVIDIA and OpenAI have pushed each other for a decade...This investment and infrastructure partnership mark the next leap forward."
- Sam Altman: "Compute infrastructure will be the basis for the economy of the future, and we will utilize what we're building with NVIDIA."
- Greg Brockman: "We've utilized their platform to create AI systems that hundreds of millions of people use every day."
- Source: Nvidia Newsroom, https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/openai-and-nvidia-announce-strategic-partnership-to-deploy-10gw-of-nvidia-systems (fetched, primary).
- Wikipedia notes that as of Jan. 2026 this MOU "has not been realized, and the two sides are rethinking the future of their partnership" — a tension point before the August guarantee.

## The $250B guarantee talks (July 2026) and structure
- Reported July 27, 2026 (CNBC/Yahoo Finance): Nvidia in talks to guarantee a $250 billion backstop enabling OpenAI and SoftBank to build a data center on federal land in Pike County, Ohio (~50 miles from Columbus). Total project cost estimated at $500 billion for 10 gigawatts.
- Structure: a debt guarantee underlying SB Energy's (SoftBank subsidiary) fundraising. Quote from reporting: "SB Energy plans to raise even more debt from unnamed lenders to fund the project, debt those lenders likely wouldn't touch without Jensen's guarantee sitting underneath it." Covers OpenAI's long-term lease/build-out costs; excludes Nvidia chip purchases (separately negotiated, estimated ~$350B).
- Japan committed $33 billion to the project; US retains 90% of power revenue after Japan recoups its investment.
- Framing as "circular financing": characterized by the reporting as "a subtle, indirect subsidy from the US government, Japan, and Nvidia."
- Source: Yahoo Finance/Barron's-syndicated piece, https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidia-guarantees-250b-openai-everyone-193929851.html (fetched); CNBC original, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/nvidia-and-openai-in-talks-for-up-to-250-billion-dollar-ai-backstop.html (found via search, fetch blocked 403).

## Scale-back and finalization
- Aug. 14, 2026: WSJ reported (via MarketScreener) Nvidia scaled the guarantee back to less than $120 billion, down from the original $250B ask, to address investor concern about risk exposure. Source: https://www.marketscreener.com/news/nvidia-scales-back-250-billion-openai-data-center-guarantee-wsj-reports-ce7859dfda88f022 (fetched). No named analyst quotes found in this piece specifically on circular financing.
- Aug. 17, 2026: Nvidia and OpenAI finalized an 8 GW data center lease in Pike County, Ohio, for a 20-year term, with Nvidia providing a $105 billion financial backstop — the actual final structure, down from the $250B figure floated in July. Source: Wikipedia OpenAI page synthesis (fetched), cross-consistent with the MarketScreener scale-back report and the userEmail-provided verified context ("finalized around Aug. 17, 2026").

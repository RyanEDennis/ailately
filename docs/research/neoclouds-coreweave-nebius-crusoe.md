## CoreWeave (rich, Wikipedia, fetched)
- IPO: March 28, 2025. Size reduced from $2.7B to $1.5B raised; ~$27 billion valuation at debut. Dealogic reportedly called it the largest AI-related listing by amount raised.
- Core Scientific: CoreWeave offered ~$1B in June 2024 (rejected); agreed to a $9 billion acquisition in July 2025; Core Scientific shareholders rejected the deal in October 2025.
- OpenAI contract: announced March 2025 ahead of the IPO. Five-year deal worth roughly $12 billion (~$11.9B some sources). OpenAI also took a $350 million stake via private placement.
- Meta: February 2026, CoreWeave sought $8.5B in financing using its Meta contract as collateral; April 2026, a $21 billion infrastructure partnership expansion was announced.
- Q1 2026 financial results: revenue $2.08 billion, beating analyst estimates of $1.97B, roughly double year-over-year. Revenue backlog near $100 billion as of Q1.
- Debt/capital: August 2023 $2.3B facility (Magnetar/Blackstone); May 2024 $1.1B round (Coatue) at $19B valuation; October 2024 $650M credit line (Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, Morgan Stanley); January 2026 Nvidia $2B investment at $87.20/share; April 2026 upsized $3.5B convertible senior notes offering.
- Leadership: CEO Michael Intrator (co-founder, 2017); CFO Nitin Agrawal (joined 2024, from Google); COO Sachin Jain (joined Aug 2024, from Oracle's AI department); CRO/Chief Strategy Officer Brian Venturo (co-founder, 2017); CTO Peter Salanki (co-founder); Chief Development Officer Brannin McBee (co-founder); SVP Engineering Chen Goldberg (joined Aug 2024, from Google).

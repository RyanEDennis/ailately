Verified facts:

- OpenAI acquired io Products (Jony Ive, Scott Cannon, Evans Hankey, Tang Tan) for $6.5B, announced May 21, 2025, closed July 9, 2025; OpenAI's Startup Fund had earlier bought a 23% stake for $1.5B late 2024; 55 employees joined; Ive and LoveFrom stayed independent. 2026: Wired reported the "io" hardware brand dropped over a trademark suit, hardware delayed to 2027. Source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Io_Products (accessed Sept 4, 2026).
- Google paid $2.4B (July 2025) to license Windsurf's tech and hire CEO Varun Mohan and co-founder Douglas Chen, after OpenAI's own planned acquisition collapsed. Source (already vetted by sibling article 07): CNBC, "Google hires Windsurf CEO Varun Mohan, others in $2.4 billion AI talent deal," July 11, 2025, https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/11/google-windsurf-ceo-varun-mohan-latest-ai-talent-deal-.html; Computerworld, July 14, 2025, https://www.computerworld.com/article/4021763/google-snatches-windsurf-execs-in-a-2-4b-deal-derailing-openais-biggest-acquisition-yet.html (frames it as derailing "OpenAI's biggest acquisition yet," no confirmed OpenAI dollar figure found this session — dropped rather than asserted with false precision).
- Cognition signed a definitive agreement in July 2025 to acquire the remaining Windsurf business after the Google fallout. Cognition reached a $10B valuation Sept 2025, $26B by May 2026, rebranded Windsurf to "Devin Desktop" June 2026, and was in talks for financing at $40B+ by Aug 2026 (Bloomberg). Source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognition_AI (accessed Sept 4, 2026).
- Meta paid $14.3B for a 49% stake in Scale AI, announced June 12-13, 2025; implied valuation $29B; installed Alexandr Wang as chief AI officer; Jason Droege became Scale AI's interim CEO. Source (already vetted by sibling articles 01/03): TechCrunch, June 13, 2025, https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/13/scale-ai-confirms-significant-investment-from-meta-says-ceo-alexandr-wang-is-leaving/; CNBC, June 12, 2025, https://www.cnbc.com/2025/06/12/scale-ai-founder-wang-announces-exit-for-meta-part-of-14-billion-deal.html.
- Nvidia agreed in December 2025 to buy assets from Groq for $20B cash plus a non-exclusive license to Groq's inference technology; several Groq senior leaders, including its CEO (Jonathan Ross), agreed to join Nvidia; both companies said Groq would continue operating independently; the structure drew criticism from industry analysts as a way to sidestep regulatory scrutiny. Source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nvidia (accessed Sept 4, 2026), citing CNBC, December 2025 (exact URL unavailable this session).
- CoreWeave agreed in July 2025 to acquire Core Scientific for $9B; Core Scientific shareholders rejected the deal in October 2025 — it never closed. (Earlier: CoreWeave's $1B 2024 offer was also rejected.) Source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CoreWeave (accessed Sept 4, 2026).
- ServiceNow agreed to buy Moveworks for "nearly $3 billion," reported by Bloomberg, March 2025. ServiceNow also acquired Cuein AI (Jan 2025), Data.World (May 2025), Traceloop (March 2025) and Pyramid Analytics (February 2026 — a genuine 2026 deal). ServiceNow struck LLM partnerships with Anthropic and OpenAI in January 2026. Source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ServiceNow (accessed Sept 4, 2026).

Confirmed announcements: "Search Agents and Generative UI" as a major Search overhaul; Gemini 3.5 Flash; Gemini Omni (multimodal, generates video output from any input) and Gemini Spark; AI-powered audio glasses with Samsung, Warby Parker and Gentle Monster slated for fall 2026; Antigravity 2.0; a Universal Commerce Protocol for agent-driven shopping. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_I/O ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_(chatbot)

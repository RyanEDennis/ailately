# Research notes: The Chief AI Officer Wave

## Survey data on CAIO prevalence
- IBM Institute for Business Value study (published May 5, 2026; surveyed 2,000 CEOs across 33 geographies, 21 industries): 76% of organizations had a Chief AI Officer role in 2026, up from 26% in 2025 — a 50-point jump. AI-first C-suite structures scaled 10% more AI initiatives than peers. 64% of CEOs say they're comfortable making major decisions on AI-generated insights; 83% call AI sovereignty critical to strategy. Workforce context: only 25% of employees use AI regularly despite 86% of CEOs believing staff already have the skills; 29% will need reskilling and 53% upskilling between 2026 and 2028. Source: IBM study covered by People Matters, https://www.peoplematters.in/news/ai-and-emerging-tech/76percent-of-firms-now-have-a-chief-ai-officer-up-from-26percent-in-a-year-ibm-49550 (fetched).
- Deloitte's "State of AI in the Enterprise" 2026 report exists (https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/what-we-do/capabilities/applied-artificial-intelligence/content/state-of-ai-in-the-enterprise.html) — found via search, not fetched; not used for a specific stat to avoid unverified figures.

## Federal CAIO mandate (primary-adjacent)
- OMB Memorandum M-25-21, "Accelerating Federal Use of AI through Innovation, Governance, and Public Trust," dated April 3, 2025, grounded in President Trump's Jan. 23, 2025 executive order "Removing Barriers to American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence." Requires agencies to appoint a Chief AI Officer within 60 days to govern "high-impact AI." CFO Act agencies must also establish an Agency AI Governance Board within 90 days and publish an AI strategy within 180 days. Source: Jones Day legal analysis, https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/05/omb-directs-agencies-to-accelerate-ai-adoption-and-devise-governance-strategy (fetched).

## Angle notes
Sector spread achieved: banking (HSBC, Commonwealth Bank, Bank of Ireland), retail (Target), healthcare/insurance (SCAN, UnitedHealth, New York Life, general insurance trend), pharma (Pfizer, institutional only), federal government (OMB mandate). Common pattern across named hires: CAIOs promoted from operating/COO roles inside the same company (Rice at HSBC) as often as recruited from outside with policy/health-system experience (Bhandari from CMS). The federal mandate (60-day appointment window) plausibly accelerated the corporate wave by normalizing the title.

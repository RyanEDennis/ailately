# Research notes: GPT-5 and the cadence behind it

- GPT-5 launched Aug. 7, 2025, per OpenAI's own developer announcement. Benchmarks cited on that page: SWE-bench Verified 74.9%, Aider polyglot 88%, tau2-bench telecom 96.7%, AIME '25 94.6%. API pricing: GPT-5 $1.25/$10 per million tokens (input/output); GPT-5 mini $0.25/$2; GPT-5 nano $0.05/$0.40; GPT-5-chat-latest (non-reasoning) $1.25/$10. Context window: 272,000 input tokens, 128,000 reasoning/output tokens, 400,000 total. https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-for-developers/
- gpt-oss (gpt-oss-120b and gpt-oss-20b), OpenAI's first open-weight release since GPT-2, shipped Aug. 5, 2025 under Apache 2.0 license, days ahead of GPT-5 itself. Confirmed via OpenAI's own "Introducing gpt-oss" page, an arXiv model card (2508.10925), and Simon Willison's Aug. 5, 2025 writeup. https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-oss/ ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10925
- OpenAI kept shipping named point updates through 2026: Wikipedia maintains standalone pages for GPT-5.1, GPT-5.2, GPT-5.3-Codex, GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.5, confirming a sustained numbered cadence continued past the initial GPT-5 launch. A trade write-up (Let's Data Science) frames GPT-5.5 as arriving roughly six weeks after GPT-5.4, evidence of a compressed release rhythm, though exact calendar dates for each point release were not independently confirmed within budget — used only to establish cadence, not pinned as specific dates in the piece. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.5 ; https://letsdatascience.com/blog/openai-gpt-5-5-six-weeks-after-5-4-doubled-price
- Jerry Tworek, OpenAI VP of Research, is leaving the company, according to Wired reporter Maxwell Zeff (Jan. 9, 2026 story, seen via Techmeme aggregation), after OpenAI leadership sided with Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki in an internal disagreement over research direction. This is a genuine research-leadership rupture, not a routine departure. https://www.techmeme.com/260108/p40 (aggregating Wired/Maxwell Zeff)
- Mark Chen holds the title Chief Research Officer at OpenAI; Jakub Pachocki holds Chief Scientist. Both appeared together in an Andreessen Horowitz podcast ("From Vibe Coding to Vibe Researching") and a July 31, 2025 MIT Technology Review profile describing them as the two people shaping OpenAI's research direction. https://www.technologyreview.com/2025/07/31/1120885/the-two-people-shaping-the-future-of-openais-research/ ; https://theorg.com/org/openai/org-chart/mark-chen
- Noam Shazeer, Google's Gemini co-lead (Transformer co-inventor, returned to Google in 2024 via the Character.AI licensing deal), left Google for OpenAI, per CNBC, June 18, 2026. Confirmed independently via search result title and matches pre-verified context. Exact title at OpenAI not confirmed within budget (fetch on cnbc.com returned a 403); avoid specifying a title, describe as joining OpenAI's research effort. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/18/google-gemini-co-lead-noam-shazeer-leaves-for-openai.html
- Compute: OpenAI and Nvidia announced a strategic partnership to deploy 10 gigawatts of Nvidia systems, per joint OpenAI and Nvidia newsroom announcements (date not independently pinned within budget; treat as a 2025-2026 era commitment, keep brief since dedicated compute pieces exist elsewhere in the plan, order 21/22). https://openai.com/index/openai-nvidia-systems-partnership/ ; https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/openai-and-nvidia-announce-strategic-partnership-to-deploy-10gw-of-nvidia-systems
- GPT-4.5 (research preview, Feb. 2025) and o3/o4-mini (reasoning models, April 2025) precede GPT-5 in the cadence; background knowledge, consistent with a dedicated GPT-4.5 Wikipedia entry surfaced in search, used only for sequencing context, not for specific disputed statistics.

Angle: GPT-5's Aug. 7, 2025 launch reads as a research-organization story as much as a model story — Pachocki and Chen consolidated authority, Tworek's exit six months later exposed the internal cost of that consolidation, and Shazeer's arrival from a rival lab shows OpenAI recruiting to backfill exactly the kind of foundational researcher a fast release cadence burns through.

## Safe Superintelligence Inc. (SSI)

- Founded by Ilya Sutskever, Daniel Gross, and Daniel Levy, June 19, 2024. Raised $1B in Sept. 2024 (Sequoia, a16z among investors); reached a $30B valuation by March 2025. Gross departed for Meta July 2025; Sutskever became CEO the same day. — Wikipedia, "Safe Superintelligence Inc." (fetched), https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safe_Superintelligence_Inc.

## Thinking Machines Lab

- Founded by Mira Murati (ex-OpenAI CTO) following her Sept. 2024 OpenAI departure; drew OpenAI alumni including John Schulman (Feb. 2025). Later lost five founding members to Meta's hiring push. — Fortune (Feb. 6, 2025, above); TheNextWeb (above).

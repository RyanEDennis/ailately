## Angle
Founder-scientist labs (single star researcher/exec as center of gravity) command enormous valuations pre-revenue/pre-product, but the same compensation war destabilizing Big Tech labs is hitting them too — Thinking Machines lost a third of its founding team within roughly a year despite a $12B valuation. The model is not immune to the dynamics it was created to escape.

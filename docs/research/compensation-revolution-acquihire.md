- March 2024: Microsoft paid $650 million to license Inflection's technology and hired nearly all ~70 employees; proceeds reimbursed Inflection's investors. ## Amazon–Adept (confirmed via TechCrunch tag page + article fetch)
- June 28, 2024: Amazon hired Adept AI's founders, including CEO David Luan. https://techcrunch.com/2024/06/28/amazon-hires-founders-away-from-ai-startup-adept/
- Dec. 9, 2024: Amazon formed a new AI-agent-focused lab (AGI Lab) led by Luan. https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/09/amazon-forms-a-new-ai-agent-focused-lab-led-by-adept-co-founder/
- Aug. 23, 2025: TechCrunch profiled Luan defending the structure as one of the first "reverse acquihires" — hiring key people and licensing tech rather than an outright acquisition. No dollar figure for the Adept deal was disclosed in any of these pieces. https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/23/amazon-agi-labs-chief-defends-his-reverse-acquihire/

## Amazon–Covariant (confirmed via TechCrunch article fetch)
- Aug. 31, 2024: Amazon hired Covariant's three founders (Pieter Abbeel, Peter Chen, Rocky Duan) plus about a quarter of its staff, and took a non-exclusive license to Covariant's robotic foundation models. Covariant continued operating independently under new CEO Ted Stinson (formerly COO) and Tianhao Zhang, serving warehouse/retail robotics customers. No dollar figure disclosed. Amazon's stated goal: embed the models into its own warehouse robot fleet. https://techcrunch.com/2024/08/31/amazon-hires-the-founders-of-robotics-ai-startup-covariant/

## Angle
The "compensation revolution" runs on two parallel tracks: eye-popping direct pay (nine-figure and reported ~$200M individual packages) for marquee researchers, and license-and-hire deal structures (Microsoft-Inflection, Amazon-Adept, Amazon-Covariant, Google-Character.AI, Google-Windsurf, Meta-Scale) that let incumbents buy an entire team's expertise while avoiding a formal acquisition and the merger review that would trigger. Regulators have looked once (UK CMA on Microsoft-Inflection) and let it through given the target's small market share — a precedent that likely emboldened the wave of similar deals that followed through 2024-2025.

- Senate voted 99-1 in July 2025 to strip a proposed 10-year moratorium on state AI laws that House Republicans had inserted into a tax-and-spending bill in May 2025. Source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regulation_of_artificial_intelligence_in_the_United_States
- President Trump signed Executive Order 14179 in January 2025, which directed development of a national "AI Action Plan." The White House/ai.gov published "America's AI Action Plan," organized around three pillars: accelerating innovation, building AI infrastructure, and leading in international diplomacy/security. Source: https://www.ai.gov/action-plan (exact July 2025 date and named authors such as David Sacks/Michael Kratsios were not confirmed in fetchable content this session — drop specific authorship claim, keep plan description general).
- Trump signed Executive Order 14365, "Ensuring a National Policy Framework for Artificial Intelligence," in December 2025, seeking to preempt state AI regulation. Source: same Wikipedia US-regulation page.
- White House released "A National Legislative Policy Framework for Artificial Intelligence: Legislative Recommendations" in March 2026 — the 2026 congressional preemption push, giving Congress recommended federal legislation. Source: same Wikipedia page.
- California: Gov. Newsom signed the Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act (SB 53, "TFAIA") in September 2025; took effect Jan. 1, 2026; first state statute specifically addressing frontier AI model development; requires safety-test disclosure and whistleblower protections. Source: same Wikipedia page.
- Colorado: original comprehensive AI Act implementation delayed from Feb. 1, 2026 to an expected June 1, 2026 start; on May 14, 2026, Gov. Polis signed SB 26-189 repealing and replacing the original act with less stringent requirements. Source: same Wikipedia page.
- New York: Gov. Hochul signed the RAISE Act (Responsible AI Safety and Education Act) Dec. 19, 2025; imposes transparency/safety duties on frontier AI developers; expected effective Jan. 1, 2027. Source: same Wikipedia page.
- Texas: Gov. Abbott signed TRAIGA (Texas Responsible AI Governance Act) June 22, 2025; effective Jan. 1, 2026; creates a Texas Artificial Intelligence Council. Source: same Wikipedia page.
- Super PAC spending: In August 2025, Silicon Valley companies/investors pledged up to $200 million combined to two pro-AI super PACs — "Meta California" (funded by Meta) and "Leading the Future." In February 2026, Anthropic donated $20 million to Public First Action, a group supporting AI-regulation-friendly political candidates — the opposite deregulatory lean from Leading the Future. Source: same Wikipedia page.
- Leading the Future super PAC (FEC ID C00916114): launched Aug. 15, 2025, Henderson, Nevada; advocates AI-industry deregulation. Major 2026 donors: Andreessen Horowitz co-founders Marc Andreessen and Ben Horowitz ($50M), OpenAI president Greg Brockman and Anna Brockman ($25M), Palantir co-founder Joe Lonsdale; total raised exceeds $140 million. Co-led by Zac Moffatt and Josh Vlasto. OpenAI Chief Global Affairs Officer Chris Lehane advised on the PAC's formation; a16z's head of government affairs Collin McCune took part in pre-launch discussions. Affiliated nonprofit Build American AI ran a "dark money" deregulation campaign. Source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leading_the_Future

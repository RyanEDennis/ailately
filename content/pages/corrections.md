AI Lately corrects errors promptly and in the open. This page is the permanent log. Each entry names the piece, describes the change, and records the date.

## Report an error

Write to corrections@ailately.com with the URL of the piece and the sentence in question. The desk acknowledges every report within two business days and resolves clear factual errors within five. A corrected piece carries a dated note describing the change. Disputed interpretations get a reply from the editor and, where warranted, a published response from the subject.

## Automated reports

Reports drafted by the automated newsdesk fall under this policy in full. A reader who spots an error in an automated piece uses the same address, and the editor can pull any automated piece immediately while the record gets checked.

Entries begin on launch day, Sept. 4, 2026, and will appear here, newest first, in this format: date, piece, what changed, and why.

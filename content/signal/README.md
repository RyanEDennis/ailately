# The Signal

The Signal tracks the biggest hires, departures, promotions, and foundings across artificial intelligence each week. Coverage spans frontier labs, infrastructure providers, applied-AI startups, enterprise leadership, and the crypto ventures building AI-native financial rails.

Every entry traces to a public source: a company newsroom, an SEC filing, or reporting from Reuters, Bloomberg, the Wall Street Journal, Financial Times, The Information, CNBC, Axios, TechCrunch, or wire services like Business Wire and PR Newswire. Personal information stays limited to name, title, employer, date, and the source link.

Three confidence labels accompany each move. **Confirmed** means a company statement, regulatory filing, or the person's own public words establish the fact directly. **Reported** means reputable journalists cite sources close to the situation, with independent outlets converging on the same details. **Inferred** marks a pattern-based read, used sparingly and flagged plainly in the accompanying analysis.

Weekly files live in `weeks/`, named by ISO week (`2026-W36.json`). Each carries a headline claim, a short essay on the week's sharpest signal, and the individual moves behind it. Accuracy governs every choice here: a quiet week stays quiet rather than padded with speculation, and a verified handful beats a dozen loosely sourced names.
